Privacy Policy

SyllaStudy AI·Effective May 1, 2026

The Short Version

We collect the minimum data needed to make SyllaStudy AI work. We don't sell it. We don't use it for ads. Here's the full picture.

What We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account data — your email address and basic profile information if you create an account or sign in
  • Content you create — notes, quiz responses, and chat messages you write within the app, stored so you can access them later
  • Usage analytics — basic, anonymized logs (pages visited, features used, error reports) to help us improve the app

We do not collect payment information, location data, or anything unrelated to the core app experience.

How We Use Your Data

Data we collect is used solely to operate and improve SyllaStudy AI. Specifically:

  • To store and retrieve your notes, quizzes, and tasks across sessions
  • To authenticate your identity when you sign in
  • To pass your messages to AI providers so they can generate responses
  • To diagnose errors and improve the stability and quality of the app

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your content to train AI models. We do not build advertising profiles or share your information with marketers — ever.

Third-Party Services

SyllaStudy AI relies on the following trusted services to function. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

SupabaseDatabase & authentication
VercelHosting & edge delivery
Mistral AIPrimary AI model
Google GeminiFallback AI model

When you use AI features, your messages are sent to these providers to generate a response. Messages are not stored by these providers beyond what's needed for the request itself.

Data Retention

We retain your account data and content for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your data deleted, contact us through the About page and we'll remove it promptly.

Anonymous usage logs are retained for up to 90 days for diagnostic purposes, then purged automatically.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data — encrypted storage via Supabase, HTTPS-only connections, and standard access controls. That said, no system is perfectly secure. We don't promise enterprise-grade compliance, but we handle your data carefully and take breaches seriously.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Just reach out through the About page. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. When we do, the effective date at the top will reflect the change. For significant updates, we may notify you within the app.

Questions about your data? We're accessible through the About page. Privacy concerns are taken seriously and responded to personally.
