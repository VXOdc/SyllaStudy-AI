What SyllaStudy AI collects and why.

We try to keep privacy simple: collect the minimum needed to run the app, never sell your data, and be honest about the third-party services that help the product work.

Last updated April 19, 2026 Transparent summary of current app behavior

Data we may collect

  • Basic account data, if account or authentication features are enabled.
  • Chats, prompts, notes, quiz content, tasks, or other workspace content that you create or save.
  • Basic usage and diagnostic logs used to keep the app available and troubleshoot problems.
  • Some feature data stored locally in your browser for faster app functionality.

How that data is used

We use data to run the product, respond to your actions, save the work you expect to keep, and maintain basic reliability and abuse protection.

We do not sell your personal data. We use it only for product functionality, service operations, and limited support or debugging needs.

Third-party services used by the app

  • Supabase may be used for database storage and authentication support.
  • Vercel hosts and serves the web application.
  • AI providers process prompts and responses. The current setup uses Mistral with a Gemini fallback path when needed.

Those providers may process data as part of delivering their part of the service. Their handling practices are governed by their own policies.

Storage approach

We keep storage practical, not excessive. Some data may live in browser storage, and some may live in supporting infrastructure only when needed to power app features or preserve saved content.

Security

We use reasonable protection measures for a modern web app, but we do not claim perfect security or enterprise-grade compliance on this page. No internet service can promise zero risk.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we may update this page at any time. The latest posted version is the version that applies to your ongoing use of the app.

If you use SyllaStudy AI through an organization or private deployment, that environment may have additional rules layered on top of this summary.
